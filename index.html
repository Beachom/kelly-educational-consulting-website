About us

A small team with a big commitment to learners.

Founded in 2013 in Oklahoma City, Kelly Educational Consulting was built on a simple belief: every learner has potential worth unlocking. We are a close-knit team, which means every client works with people who know their story.

Whether you are a family navigating the next step, an educator looking for support, or a school planning for growth, we bring practical guidance and genuine care to the table.
