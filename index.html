About us

A family-led team with a big commitment to learners.

Founded in 2013 in Oklahoma City, Kelly Educational Consulting was built on a simple belief: every learner has potential worth unlocking. What started as a passion for helping students succeed has grown into a full-service consultancy supporting schools, educators, and families.

We are a close-knit team, which means every client works with people who know their story. Our consultants work directly in classrooms and with students, backed by a leadership, marketing, events, and technology team that keeps every engagement running smoothly.

How we work

Our approach

  1. 01

    Listen & evaluate

    We learn about your learners, goals, and challenges, and observe the learning environment firsthand.

  2. 02

    Design a plan

    Together we build a clear, realistic plan with measurable academic and behavioral milestones.

  3. 03

    Coach & support

    Our consultants work alongside teachers and students with regular check-ins and adjustments.

  4. 04

    Measure & grow

    We review results, celebrate progress, and set the next goal, because potential keeps growing.

FAQ

Common questions

Who do you work with?

We work with schools and districts, individual teachers, and students and their families, primarily in the Oklahoma City area.

What happens in a first consultation?

We talk through your goals and challenges, answer your questions, and recommend next steps. If we are a good fit, we will outline a tailored plan and timeline.

Do you come to our school?

Yes. Learning-environment evaluations and teacher coaching happen on-site so we can see and support the real classroom.

Can you help with both academics and behavior?

Absolutely. Our consultants coach teachers and students to improve academic and behavioral outcomes, because the two go hand in hand.

How do I get started?

Send us a message using the form below and a member of our team will be in touch to schedule a consultation.
